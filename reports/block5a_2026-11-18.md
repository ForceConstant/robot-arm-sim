## 1. Plan limit fix

The ChArUco plan's `joints` are simulator radians; all 11 poses, and all 32
recovery poses, commanded `wrist_roll −87.21°` against the bridge's
`−81.82..91.05°`. Clamped every joint into the live limits with a 0.30° inside
margin, reading the limits from `GET /state` (no motion):

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| `wrist_roll` (11 poses + 32 recovery) | −87.21° | **−81.52°** |
| worst pose margin to any limit | −5.39° (violation) | **+0.30°** |
| gripper | 78.5 (command units)… | 78.5; the sim jaw maps to **100.0**, exactly on the 0..100 bridge bound |
| ids / offset | 5–36 / 0.0 | unchanged |
| joints changed | — | 43 |

Clamping, rather than re-solving, is the route the task allowed. The planner's
IK has no limit bound (`solve_pose` runs unconstrained MuJoCo), so a
limit-constrained regeneration is a planner change, not a bench change. A
5.4° tool-roll clamp is a rotation about the tool axis; it does not change which
part of the board the camera looks at (verified below: identical tag sets at
−87.2° and −81.52° at three poses).

**New hard gate in the validator.** `tools/validate_scan_plan.py` gained
`--limits` (inline JSON / file) and `--bridge-limits URL` (GET only). When
supplied it asserts every pose *and* recovery pose is inside the limits, prints
the worst margin, records `joint_limit_check` in the report, and exits 1 on a
violation — so an unexecutable plan fails in sim, not at the arm.

### Findings

1. **The plan's authored `visible` lists are stale.** Rendering the *current*
   sim wrist camera at each plan pose detects 0–5 tags (union 11), while the
   plan claims 4–15 per pose. The plan was authored against the pre-4c camera
   (`fx 738.6`) and was never re-solved after the lens/mount correction; 4c's
   "plan still valid" checked the *union* over all 11 poses (10–13), which
   hides that most individual poses see nothing.
2. **The roll clamp is not the cause.** marker71/marker37/marker57 render the
   identical tag sets at `−87.2°` and `−81.52°`.
3. **Sim and IRL disagree at identical joints.** At marker50 the sim sees 0 but
   the arm saw 3; at marker37 the sim sees 5 and the arm saw 0. The real view
   is roughly one pose-region away from the model's. The plan's tag margins are
   also at the frame border (`edge_px 56.8` on a 640×480 frame), so a few
   degrees of aim error — or a 1.4–2.2° settling residual — drops them.
4. **Pose tracking is marginal at these postures.** 7 of 11 poses never settled
   inside the controller's tolerance in 12 s (2.13–2.26° residual; the four that
   passed sat at 0.57–1.42°). Everything was commanded at `speed_scale 0.3`;
   nothing was clamped, rejected, or faulted. The scan plan's postures are
   oblique (view pitch **132.4°**, i.e. 47.6° off nadir) and 250 mm above the
   board, while every calibrated view is near-nadir at 150–170 mm (pitch
   178–179°).
5. **Conclusion:** the 4c hand-eye mount reproduces the real camera at the
   calibration volume (1.25 px median) but not at the scan's 250 mm / 47°
   oblique volume. A joint-space plan built for that volume cannot register,
   and the plan's per-pose coverage was never checked with real margins.

## 5. Recommendation (planner)

1. **Regenerate the plan against the current camera** (`reports/block4c_sim_calibration.json`)
   with the bridge limits applied from the start, and require **per-pose**
   coverage: ≥3 tags per pose with a real frame margin (≥12 px), not a union.
2. **Solve in the volume that can be executed.** Either keep the plan near-nadir
   in the calibrated 150–170 mm band, or extend the hand-eye solve and the
   view-match gate to the 250 mm / 47° oblique volume before trusting a
   joint-space plan there.
3. **Settling:** the arm needs >1.5° at these postures. Fix the tracking (load,
   torque limits, command ramp) or plan postures that settle — do **not** widen
   `pose_tolerance_deg`.
