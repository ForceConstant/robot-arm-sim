## 3. h8 classification: **seed basin, not reach and not model asymmetry**

The first sweep after Block 1 confirmed h8 = 20.8 mm (0.8 mm outside the gate),
a8 = 18.5 mm. Two candidate explanations were tested:

1. **Model asymmetry** — rejected. Mirror split 2.3 mm; rotation sign has no
   effect; no limit pinned.
2. **Pure reach** — also rejected:
   * `tools/reach_envelope.py --limits calibration` (march +y at x=98, z=60)
     reaches the target region: 9.0 mm at y=90, 10.1 mm at y=94, **12.40 mm at
y=98** (the h8 target), with wrist_flex at its bounded maximum (93.4° = 95°
     limit − 1.6°).
   * A bounded local solve from a flex 90/92/93/93.4 seed returns **12.20 mm**
     at joints `14.1, 59.2, −64.7, 93.4, 22.4` — i.e. **inside the real limits**.
   * The unbounded walk only does marginally better (12.3 mm at flex 96.4),
     so the constrained optimum is ~12.2 mm, not ~20.8 mm.

**Actual cause:** `ArmKinematics.solve_tip`'s escalation seeded only the
*folded* arm family — `[pan, −55, +72, +39, ...]` plus a coarse grid whose lift
never exceeds +20 and whose elbow is never negative. The far +y corners need
the *extended* branch (lift ≈ +58, elbow ≈ −64, wrist_flex ≈ +93). From the
home seed the best of 85 starts was therefore 20.8 mm: a local minimum, not a
physical limit.

### Fix (one change, in `live/kinematics.py`)

`solve_tip` now also seeds the extended branch:

```python
for pan in (0.0, -20.0, 20.0, -45.0, 45.0):
    seeds.append([pan, 58.0, -64.0, 90.0, 0.0, seed[5]])
```

Escalation still replaces the fast result only when it is 5 mm better, so this
can add candidates but cannot discard a valid basin. Gate, tip constant and
tolerance are unchanged.
