## 1. What changed

- **Pre-filter removed.** It scored every view at a seed that is 0.3° off, so it
  deleted two healthy 8 px views and kept a different 20 than the truth-based
  set (block 5i: `obs=18`).
- **Absolute 8 px prune limit** (was `median + 3·MAD`, then a capped variant):
  on this bench real views sit at 1–3 px, so the two gross outliers
  (787.66 px, 10.44 px) are the only ones pruned. The module now keeps the same
  20 views as the direct computation.
- **Robust first pass** (`soft_l1`, f_scale 3) so one 787 px detection cannot
  drag the pose out from under the pruner.
- **Refit from multiple starts**: the robust result, every finite PnP seed, and
  a deterministic 7×7×5 coarse grid over the allowed box, keeping the lowest
  residual.

## 2. The finding that matters

With the same 20 views and the same 4c pose, two numbers disagree:

| computed where | residual at the truth pose |
|---|---|
| inside `estimate_board_pose` (`error([-0.00992,-0.00313,0.09896])`, 20 views, 240 corners) | **4.547 px** |
| directly from the same arrays, same `_project`, same `marker_z` (`/tmp/b5_floor.py`) | **1.38 px** |

Per-view median over all 22 frames is **1.32 px**, and the concat over the 20
inliers is **1.38 px**. So the correct answer is reachable — but the function's
own `error()` is roughly 3× larger than the residual of the same data.

**That is the bug: `error()` is not composing what I think it is.** The four
candidates are the point/pixel pairing, the per-view `marker_z`, the corner
order, or a duplicated observation. The next step is to dump **one**
observation from inside the function (first 4 object points, first 4 pixels,
its calibration) and compare it with the direct build — a five-line diff once
the arrays are side by side.

The function still returns `None` at 1.2 px; its best fit is
(−8.94, −3.80) mm, yaw 5.39°, **3.008 px**.

## 3. Retraction

In 5h/5i I reported **1.321 px** as "the floor". That number came from my replay
script, whose seed ranking was affected by a NaN sort. It is **not reproducible
from the module**, and the real floor over this data is **1.38 px** at the truth
pose. The claim that the estimator is "0.12 px from the gate" was therefore
wrong: the function is ~1.6 px away and its objective is inconsistent with the
data.
