## 3. Why this is not a gate problem

- The gate is 1.5 px and the cal frames fuse at **1.322 px** with the same code,
  the same camera and the same plane.
- The scan views carry **1 tag each**, and a single-tag view is rotationally
  ambiguous: the fusion gets one bad view and the fit goes to 40 px.
- The plan was validated against `observe_markers`, the *model* of detection. The
  model says 3–5 tags per pose; the real detector yields ~1. The 43 % yield
  measured in 5e (62 of 144 model-visible tags) is the same effect, and at these
  poses it is worse still.

So the binding constraint is **real per-view detection**, not `MAX_RMS_PX` and
not the fusion.

## 4. What would fix it

1. **More tags per view.** Either many more poses per board region (a plan with
   ≥2–3× the poses so each region is seen repeatedly) or a smaller standoff with
   a wider lens. With ~1 tag/view, no fusion works.
2. **Detection yield.** Focus was healthy (`/focus-score` 144) and the exposure
   correlation is retracted, but 1 tag out of 3–5 visible is low. Worth checking
   a single captured frame by eye at a scan pose: if the tags are sharp and the
   detector still misses them, the detector's thresholds are the problem, not
   the optics.
3. **Accept single-tag views** with a per-view pose prior (the camera pose is
   known from FK, so a single tag *can* constrain the board — but that is a new
   estimator, not a gate).

I did not change any of those; they are planning decisions.
