# Block 5c — occlusion model audited against real frames (2026-11-18)

**Status: STOPPED at step 1 — the occlusion model cannot be validated against the
real frames, so no plan was regenerated and nothing was scanned or swept.**

The measurement below is the reason: with the **installed CAD parallel tool** the
mask covers **48.2 % of every frame** and covers **52 % of the tags the real
camera actually decoded**. The real frame at that posture shows board across the
whole image and no tool at all, while the sim view at the same joints puts the
tool over the right half. That is a model/installation mismatch, not a
conservative margin.

## 1. Occlusion model — root cause

Audit script: every non-duplicate `_cal` frame with an image (35 frames), the
recorded joints, the live calibration and `board.json`:

| measure | value |
|---|---|
| frames | 35 |
| tags decoded by the real detector | 62 |
| tags projected fully in frame (model) | 144 |
| **detector yield** | **43 %** |
| **mask coverage, every frame** | **48.2 % (constant)** |
| **decoded tags the mask suppresses (>2 % overlap)** | **32/62 = 52 %** |
| reprojection RMS of decoded corners | median **1.32 px** (one bad frame at 782 px) |

The constant 48.2 % is the tell: the tool is rigidly mounted next to the lens, so
its silhouette is fixed in the camera frame — but the real images do not contain
that block. Side-by-side at `20261117T120835104_cal`:

- **Sim + mask:** grey gripper across the right half, red mask over it (48.2 %).
- **Real frame:** ChArUco board edge to edge, no gripper anywhere in frame.

A tag the real camera **decoded** (id 10) is **99.9 % covered** by the mask —
a certain false positive, not a borderline one.

**Why the earlier 16 % reading was wrong:** `SelfOcclusionModel` selects its
geometry from `ROBOT_GRIPPER`; my first audit ran without
`ROBOT_GRIPPER=cad_parallel` and measured the *stock* jaw (16 %, also constant).
The planner always runs with the CAD pack, i.e. the 48 % mask. The correct
configuration is the one above.

**Precise mismatch to fix:** the model's camera↔tool relation puts the installed
tool across half the view; the real camera does not see it. So either the tool
mount/orientation in the model is wrong (the same 178°-convention family as the
4c mount fix), or the installed pack differs from what the model loads. Until
that is resolved the mask vetoes poses the hardware can actually use, and
planning with it is what produced 0 poses at 150/160/170 mm.

Secondary, independent of the pack: the **2 % overlap threshold** rejects 52 % of
genuinely decodable tags. Even with correct geometry that rule is far stricter
than the detector (ArUco decodes through partial occlusion). Quantified, not
changed.

## 2. Settling — measured, and a working fix

**Longer hold does nothing.** 60 consecutive target sends over 6 s at speed 0.3:
the elbow sat at **1.57° short** and did not move (0.00° drift over 5 s). So the
servo has no integral action; repeating the target is inert.

**A residual nudge works.** Commanding `target + residual`:

| step | command | measured | error |
|---|---|---|---|
| plain target | −65.0 | −63.43 | 1.57 |
| correction | −66.6 | −64.66 | 1.94 |
| plain target again | −65.0 | **−64.66** | **0.34** |

One correction round cut the elbow residual **1.57 → 0.34°** (4.6×).

Implemented in `live/scan_controller.py`: `_goto` now finishes at a settled pose
with `_hold_and_correct()` — `hold_s` (2.0 s) of repeated sends, then up to
`correction_rounds` (2) of `target + residual`, clamped to the bridge limits.
`pose_tolerance_deg` stays **1.5** and the scan gate is untouched; this only
makes the arm actually arrive. Numbers: `reports/block5c_settle_hold.json`.

Note: `live/tests/test_scan_controller.py` is **4 failed / 4 passed** — the same
4 failures occur with this change stashed. They come from the **5a clamped
plan** used by the fixture: its poses now decode 2 tags in sim. Pre-existing
regression from 5a, not from this change.

## 3. Focus / exposure — quantified, secondary

| band (mean brightness) | frames | sharpness (2nd-diff var) | yield |
|---|---|---|---|
| < 70 | 12 | 145.0 | **51 %** |
| 70–90 | 4 | 88.1 | 44 % |
| 90–110 | 10 | 55.5 | 24 % |
| 110–130 | 9 | 36.7 | 30 % |

`corr(brightness, sharpness) = −0.84`, `corr(brightness, yield) = −0.42`,
`corr(sharpness, yield) = +0.48`; 13 of 35 frames decode nothing.
`/focus-score` now **144.3** (121.5 earlier in the session) — healthy.

Reading: the bright frames have lost contrast (−0.84), and they decode at half
the rate of the dim, high-contrast frames. This is exposure/glare, not defocus —
a washed-out board still looks "sharp" only if contrast survives, and here it
does not. **Physical adjustment needed:** diffuse the light on the board / avoid
direct sun and check the camera's exposure-gain, then re-capture. This is a bench
action, not a plan change; no threshold was touched.
