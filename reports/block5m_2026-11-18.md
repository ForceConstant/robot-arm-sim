# Block 5m — scan frames: the board is in view, the tags are blurred (2026-11-18)

**Status: diagnostic complete. The failing poses are not "board out of view" and
not a mount problem — the board is in frame at all five poses with 4–5 projected
tags, but three of them are blurred or grazing and decode nothing.**

- Host: BC-250, CachyOS; repo from `cac3aa5`
- `progress_card` unavailable in this subagent session.

## 1. Method

Five scan poses driven through the app's `/move` (joint targets from the 13-pose
plan), then one frame each from the bridge's `/capture`, saved as
`live/captures/scan_5m_<pose>.jpg` with the measured joints. Measured joints were
within **0.38°** of target on every pose.

Montage: **`reports/block5m_scan_frames.png`** (downloadable from the repo).

## 3. What the frames show

- **marker14**: the board fills the frame, tags crisp, three decoded.
- **marker26 / marker24**: board clearly in frame, tags large (~100 px) but
  **soft — visibly blurred**, 0 decoded.
- **marker15**: board visible but at a **grazing angle** (board edge across the
  frame), tags foreshortened and blurred, 0 decoded.
- **marker23**: one tag sharp enough to decode (24), the rest soft.

So the failure is **image quality at those poses** — defocus/blur and a grazing
incidence — not the plan's reachability, not the mount, and not the mask.

## 4. What this means

The plan's poses are geometrically valid (4–5 tags in frame, settled to 0.38°),
but three of them look at the board through a blurred or very oblique view. That
is the same effect that produced ~1 tag per view in the 5l scan:

- the 5g generator accepted poses whose *camera orientation* reached the target
  even when the board was seen at a steep incidence;
- at 160 mm the fixed-focus lens gives sharp tags only over part of that range
  (marker14 is sharp; its neighbours at the same standoff are not, which points
  at incidence/field curvature more than distance).

Both are planning/optics decisions, and I changed neither.

## 5. What I did not change

Plan, detector, gates, `MAX_RMS_PX`, lighting. No `/sanity`, no sweep.

## 6. Suggested next step (planner)

1. Add an **incidence / sharpness gate to the plan generator**: reject a pose
  whose board normal makes more than ~35–40° with the camera axis, and prefer
  poses whose predicted tag contrast is high. The 13-pose plan has at least
  three poses that fail this on the bench.
2. Re-capture the same five poses with the lens focused at the working distance
  and compare sharpness — marker14 at 180 vs 39 is a big enough gap to be worth
  one controlled A/B before touching the detector.
