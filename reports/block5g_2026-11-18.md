## 1. Mask fixed from the CAD (pass condition met)

Root cause found in `profile.json`'s own assumptions: *"CAD SIM_CAMERA_CENTER
connector transforms to (−60, +80, 0) mm in gripperframe"*. The tool pack places
the tool relative to **its** lens, not the installed one:

| | mm in `so101:gripper` |
|---|---|
| CAD lens through the exported mount | **(80.0, 60.0, −0.1)** |
| measured lens (4c, validated 1.32 px) | **(67.66, −2.92, −10.88)** |
| **offset** | **(−12.35, −62.94, −10.82), 65.0 mm** |

65 mm is exactly the displacement that put tool surfaces 60–140 mm in front of
the lens. `SelfOcclusionModel` now anchors the tool at the **measured** lens
(`lens_anchor_m`, `cad_lens_mm`, `anchor_tool_to_measured_lens`), keeping the
CAD's tool↔lens relation. Not a pixel fit, not a ruler.

Re-audit, 35 usable frames, CAD pack:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| mask coverage | 48.2 % every frame | **0.0 % mean, 0.0 % max** |
| decoded tags suppressed | 32/62 (52 %) | **0/62 (0 %)** |

Pass condition met on both counts.

## 3. On the arm — every pose now reaches and captures

Two controller bugs fixed on the way (both measured):

- `_goto` **never applied the correction on a timeout** — it returned false
  before `_hold_and_correct` ran, so a joint that settles short was abandoned.
  The timeout path now corrects and re-evaluates.
- `DEFAULT_POSE_TIMEOUT_S` 12 → **45 s**: at speed 0.3 the arm moves ~3.15 °/s,
  so a 90° reorientation needs ~29 s. The first pose of the new plan was 16.3°
  short after 12 s.

Result: **13/13 poses reached and captured**, settle errors **0.86–1.45°** (all
inside the 1.5° gate), **13 distinct markers** seen. First time a scan has
executed end to end on the arm.
