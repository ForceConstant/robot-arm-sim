## Diagnosis

- The image alone puts the real camera **174.8 mm** above the marker plane at the reference pose (per-marker ranges 180.5, 174.3, 182.8, 178.9 mm; fit rms 0.2 mm).
- The sim puts its camera **152.7 mm** above the same plane at the same joint vector: **+22.1 mm**, ratio 0.8733 (12.7% short).
- The control view confirms it: median 22.5 px, and scale 0.8817 removes all but 1.73 px.
- That is why fovy 42.03 -> 47.0 made the renders agree: tan(23.5)/tan(21.01) = 1.124,
  the same 12-13% as the range error. The FOV change compensated a geometry error.
- Intrinsics are not the cause: a 12.7% focal error is outside every solve
  (813.8 / 831.9 / 842.5 px across the models), and the rendered marker sides already
  match the IRL to 0.7% at the fitted FOV.

Order of diagnosis: **tool geometry** is the earliest stage that cannot hold. The
failing quantity is the camera-to-plane distance (22.1 mm short), which is degenerate
between the camera standoff in the tool (chain + the mount's +15 mm optical-axis shift,
which is a fit, not a CAD measurement) and the board plane z (431.35 mm, never fitted).
Intrinsics: cleared. Mount orientation: cleared at the reference pose (fit rotation
0.87 deg). Board x/y/yaw: cleared at the reference pose.

## Correction, 2026-11-19 18:28 — the 22.1 mm range error is withdrawn

After the gate failed, bench measurements (tape, calipers, and a jaw-on-board
contact datum) tested the geometry stage by stage. Every length checked out:

| check | model | measured |
|---|---|---|
| base mesh top above the table | 102.0 | 102 |
| ChArUco square pitch | 28.00 | 28.0 |
| board slab thickness | 3.20 | 3.2 |
| camera-to-tip span | 105.2 | 105.0 |
| hand, tip to wrist mount | 110.45 | 112 |
| shoulder_lift axis above the table | 149.0 | 152 |
| upper arm / forearm horn centres | 116.00 / 135.00 | check out |
| wrist_flex to tip | 172.50 | 170 |

The vertical offset against the same joints then flipped sign:

| pose | offset (real − model) |
|---|---|
| cal pose (elbow 76, lift −55) | +19.2 mm |
| reference pose (image scale) | +20.5 mm |
| jaw resting on the board (elbow −45, lift 49) | −23.9 mm |

No zero or link error has that sign structure. The measured cause is servo
deadband/hysteresis on `shoulder_lift` (receipt: `reports/b5b/deadband.json`):
the same target settles 3.78 deg apart depending on the approach direction
(0.00 deg spread within a direction, 3 repeats each), which is 14 mm at the tip
at this extension; a hand test moves the tip ~20 mm.

Consequences, and what this report retracts:

- The earlier "sim camera 22.1 mm / 13 % short of the real range" is **not** a
  geometry finding. It sits inside the deadband and is withdrawn.
- The Block 5b gate criterion (median 2 px, max 4 px, about 2 mm at 175 mm) is
  an order of magnitude tighter than this arm's open-loop repeatability
  (±15–20 mm at the tip). It cannot be met while the "same joints" are taken
  from the bridge.
- The baked `fovy` 47.0 matches the average sag at the reference pose, not a
  stable measurement. Left in place by Brian's ruling (2026-11-19 18:26),
  provisional.
- Fine placement cannot rely on calculated positions: it needs closed-loop
  control on the wrist camera. One-directional approaches remove the
  hysteresis but not the load-dependent sag.
- No zero, geometry, mount, calibration or gate constant was changed.

Measurement procedure: `tools/b5b_deadband.py`.
