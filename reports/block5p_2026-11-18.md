# Block 5p — multi-view test: the board lands ~50 mm from board.json (2026-11-18)

**Status: decisive test run. Both bases fuse far from `board.json`, so the 4c
camera model does not extrapolate to these scan postures. No plan/gate change,
no sweep.**

- Host: BC-250, CachyOS; repo from `b68e8db`
- `progress_card` unavailable in this subagent session.

## 1. Method

At each base pose: move to the plan's joints, then six views at ±10 mm in x/y
(the base plus `+10,0`, `−10,0`, `0,+10`, `0,−10`, `+10,+10`) through the app's
`/move xyz` (keep-in enforced, speed 0.3), one bridge capture each, saved as
`live/captures/scan_5p_<base>_<i>.jpg`. Each set fused with the production
`estimate_board_pose` at the 1.5 px gate and the corrected plane (0.43135).

## 2. Result

| base | views | tags/view | fusion | recovered board | rms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| marker14 | 6 | 1–3 | **rejected** | (−51.9, −10.9) mm, yaw −3.9° | **21.640 px** |

Both recovered poses sit **~45–60 mm from `board.json`**, in the same direction as
the single-view PnP from 5o ((−57.1, −10.0, yaw −4.3°)). Two independent bases
and the single view agree; that is no longer a single-view bias.

The fits are poor (20–22 px) because each view carries only 1–3 tags at the
frame border — but the *displacement* is consistent, and that is the measurement
the test was for.

## 3. Conclusion

**The 4c camera model does not extrapolate to the scan postures.** At the
calibration postures the same model reproduces the board to **0.14 mm**; at
`marker14` — whose joints are close to the calibration family (pan 11.7, lift
−65.2, elbow 80.6, wflex 44.4, roll 21.5) — the images put the board **~50 mm
away**. So the mismatch is not "the plan aimed at a corner"; it is that the
camera's modelled pose diverges from reality as the arm leaves the calibration
set.

That also explains the whole trail of this block: the projected tags landing on
the table in 5o, the 0–1 tags per view in 5l, and the 5m soft/grazing frames —
the model is drawing the board in the wrong place, so the plan's "3 tags in
frame" never corresponded to real tags.

## 4. Proposed correction (planner)

1. **Hand-eye re-solve over the working volume** — the calibration set is
   clustered near one posture family; add poses spanning pan ±40°, lift −80…+40°,
   wrist_flex 20…95° and re-solve `camera_in_tool`, then re-validate on held-out
   views. This is the root fix.
2. **Or per-pose visual servo** — solve the board pose from the tags actually in
   view at each pose and accept the measured aim, rather than trusting FK.
3. Either way the plan's coverage numbers must be recomputed from the corrected
   model before another scan.
