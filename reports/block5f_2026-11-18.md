## 2. Model camera check — it is the 4c mount

`SelfOcclusionModel` projects through `self.calibration.camera(joints)` and the
live calibration is `.private/phase11d/calibration.json`, whose
`camera_in_tool` translation is **(67.66, −2.92, −10.89) mm** — the 4c mount,
unchanged. So the mask is *not* using a stale or stock camera: it uses the 4c
camera and the 4c board projection validates at 1.32 px. The fault is the
**tool's placement relative to that camera**.

## 3. Off-board frames — why the fit cannot be made

The seven off-board frames *do* contain hardware, but not in a form that can be
fitted against the CAD tool:

- the sim scene at those joints renders a plain brown tabletop — the real bench
  has printer parts, a white perforated printed bracket, a black cable and
  hard shadows. **There is no common background**, so a silhouette IoU has
  nothing to lock onto;
- the visible hardware is a **white perforated printed part** plus a black
  shadowed mass, not a clean black CAD parallel gripper. Segmentation of "tool
  pixels" is ambiguous between the bracket, the gripper and its shadow;
- one frame (`…121025136_cal`) is dominated by a shadow across half the image.

I could not produce a fit with a meaningful residual, and I am not going to
invent a translation to hit the pass condition. **This needs a ruler (or the CAD
bracket dimension): lens front element to the nearest tool face along the view
axis.** The model currently puts tool surfaces **60–140 mm in front of the lens**;
the real frames require them behind it.

Also worth confirming while the ruler is out: the off-board frames show a white
printed bracket, so it is not certain the installed tool is the
`cad_parallel` pack the model loads. If it is a different bracket, the pack is
the wrong geometry entirely.

## 4. 140 mm probe — the requested standoff, measured

| | result |

`reports/block5f_probe_140mm.json` (the 160 mm probe is restored in
`reports/block5c_occlusion_probe.json`).

So 140 mm does **not** help: closer to the board means fewer, larger tags fit in
a 42° frame — the unmasked median falls from 4 (160 mm) to 3 (140 mm). With the
mask as it stands, every pose is at 0–2.

## 5. What this means

- Adding poses at 140 mm cannot satisfy 3 tags / 12 px **while the mask vetoes
  half the view**; the mask is the binding constraint, not the pose budget.
- Even with a corrected mask, a 140 mm view yields a median of 3 tags, so
  single-pose coverage would sit exactly on the gate — a larger plan (more
  poses, each needing ≥3) would be fragile. 160 mm has more margin (unmasked
  median 4) and is the better standoff if the mask is fixed.
- No `/scan`, no `/sanity`, no dry run, no sweep. Gates unchanged: 20 mm,
  3 tags / 12 px, `pose_tolerance_deg` 1.5.
