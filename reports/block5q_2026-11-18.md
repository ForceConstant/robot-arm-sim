## 2. Direct rotation test — not 90°

Rendered the sim wrist camera at the 5m measured joints and compared the **tag
corner orientation** (the angle of corner 0→1, which is what a roll changes)
with the real frames:

| pose | common tags | real-vs-sim tag rotation |
|---|---|---|
| marker14 | 23 | **+6.7°** |
| marker26 / 24 / 15 / 23 | none in both | — |

A 90° mismatch would show as ±90° here; it shows **6.7°**. Consistent with the
4c origin: that calibration matched the real corners at 1.25 px on 12 views, and
a 90° roll could not do that.

## 3. What that means

- The image-orientation hypothesis is **not** supported: no rotation is dropped

- The 5p result therefore stands — the ~50 mm board displacement at scan
  postures is a real pose-dependent geometry error, not an image-frame
  convention.
- **Separate question, still open:** your 90° observation is about the *rendered
