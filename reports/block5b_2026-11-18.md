## 2. Settling — diagnosed, and it is gravity/compliance

Measured at the parked 5a posture (commanded plan target vs bridge feedback):

| joint | target | measured | error |
|---|---|---|---|
| shoulder_pan | −21.26 | −21.01 | −0.25 |
| shoulder_lift | 32.81 | 33.36 | −0.55 |
| **elbow_flex** | **−68.92** | **−66.77** | **−2.15** |
| wrist_flex | 95.00 | 94.46 | 0.54 |
| wrist_roll | −81.52 | −81.14 | −0.38 |
| gripper | 78.50 | 49.43 | (released in HOLD) |

The 2.13–2.26° timeouts are the **elbow**, one joint, not the whole arm.

Follow-up moves (all inside keep-in, speed 0.3):

| command | reported error_deg | measured elbow 2 s later |
|---|---|---|
| −65.0 | 0.54 | −65.54 |
| −63.6 | 0.55 | −63.96 |
| −65.0 (return) | 0.35 | **−63.96** — did not move back |

- A 1.0° command settled 0.54° short, then the joint **crept back** to where it
  started once the stream stopped. `shoulder_lift` also drifted 33.4 → 38.7°
  during the session.
- The app's stream stops inside a 0.7° band, so part of the residual is the
  controller's own deadband — but the creep afterwards is load, not quantisation.

Verdict: gravity/compliance. A final correction (command = target + residual)
cannot hold a joint that keeps creeping away from its setpoint, so I did not
patch `scan_controller._goto`. What would help, in order: more torque/current
limit on the elbow, a slower final approach with a longer **hold** (re-sending
the target for several seconds, not a 0.6 s settle), then a correction term.
None of that is a gate change; `pose_tolerance_deg` was left at 1.5°.

## 3. Keep-in state found and fixed

After the 5a scan the arm was parked with the jaw **tip at y 118.6 mm against a
±118 mm box**, so every move was refused (`keep-in zone: y 122 outside ±118`).
Computed the recovery offline and moved the elbow −65.8 → −65.0 → −63.96,
bringing the tip to y 115.3 mm, inside the box. Arm returned to `HOLD`
(`commands_applied 2932`, `commands_rejected 0`, no fault).

Related reporting inconsistency worth fixing: `GET /keep-in` computes `inside`
from the **tool** frame while `_keep_in_check` evaluates the **jaw tip**, so the
endpoint can report `inside:true` while the guard refuses the move (exactly what
happened).
