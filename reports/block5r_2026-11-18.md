## 3. Why it cannot be placed from this file

The old jaw is placed in the tool model by **two** transforms:

- body `gripper_jaw_1__solid1` `pos="0.00487916 -0.0414625 0.00812691"`
  `quat="0.5 -0.5 0.5 0.5"` (and the mirrored right jaw
  `pos="0.0419792 0.0225375 0.00812691" quat="0.5 0.5 0.5 -0.5"`);
- geom `pos="0.022 -0.0187 0.019" quat="0 -0.707107 -0 0.707107"`.

That geom quat is the only 90° in play, and changing it alone does not work:
setting the two jaw geoms to identity produced a **broken render** (striped
surfaces across half the frame — degenerate normals/geometry), while leaving it
as-is reproduces the current side-on tool with the new mesh.

The Onshape assembly's jaw occurrences (±90° about z, per Brian) and the mapping
from the exported part's frame into the assembly jaw frame are **not derivable
from one STL**. Placing it needs either:

1. the assembly re-export with the jaw occurrences included (mesh already in the
   jaw's local frame), or
2. the per-jaw transform from Onshape (`occurrenceTransform` for both jaws), plus
   the part's own origin.

With either of those, the change is a mesh swap plus those two numbers — no
rotation guessing.
