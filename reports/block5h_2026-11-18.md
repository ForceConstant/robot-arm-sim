## 4. Decision needed

The real bench fuses at **1.32 px**; Block 3 validated the pipeline in sim at
≤0.26 mm with a synthetic camera and 4c's own derivation reported a **1.53 px**
median over 256 corners. The 1.2 px gate is therefore below what this
calibration achieves on real corners. Either:

1. accept ~1.35 px as the bench accuracy (planner decision, with the 4c number
   as precedent), or
2. improve the corner data — larger tags per view (160 mm gives 104 px edges,
   already large), more tags per view (3–5 today), or better focus/exposure —
   until the fused residual is under 1.2.
