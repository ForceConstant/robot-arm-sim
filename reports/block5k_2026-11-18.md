## 1. The finding

There was never a composition bug. The **plane was wrong**:

| source | marker plane |
|---|---|
| `live_config.json` `marker_z_m` (what the app passed) | **0.4337 m** |
| `live.json` board z, the measured 4c value | **0.43135 m** |

A 2.35 mm plane error at a 160 mm standoff is exactly the ~3× residual
inflation I measured: at the truth pose the function's `error()` gave
**4.547 px** with 0.4337 and **1.38 px** with 0.43135 on the same arrays. That
is why the module and the direct build disagreed — they were projecting the
corner set onto two different planes, not composing them differently.
