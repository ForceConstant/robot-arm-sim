## 2. Single-view check at the one good pose

`marker14`, three decoded tags, single-view PnP (RMS 0.88 px):

```
board from this frame : x −57.1 mm   y −10.0 mm   yaw −4.31°
board.json            : x  −9.9 mm   y  −3.1 mm   yaw  5.67°
```

A **47 mm / 10°** disagreement. **Caveat that matters:** three near-planar tags
in one view are weakly constrained in depth/out-of-plane rotation, so this is
suggestive rather than proof — the calibration solve used 22 views and recovered
board.json to 0.14 mm. It is the kind of bias a single view can produce.

## 3. What this means

- Not the joint chain: incidence is nadir and the 5n jaw/settle work ruled out
  the mechanics.
- The **camera↔board relation at these postures** is what disagrees: at the cal
  postures the same model reproduces the tags to 1.32 px, and here the projected
  tags are displaced by >100 px.
- Either the 4c mount does not extrapolate to these postures (they are further
  out in pan/lift than the cal set), or board.json is stale for the current
  board placement.
