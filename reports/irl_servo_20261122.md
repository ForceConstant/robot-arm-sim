# IRL close-then-servo attempt on e5 (2026-11-22)

## Summary

Mount-free gaze servo ran on the live rig (no gripper actuation). It drove the
wrist camera to bring the piece on e5 to the centre of the frame, but stopped on
a reach 409 before the numeric gaze error reached tolerance. No transform was
used: perception and error path were PnP-only (`Board.solve`, `unproject`);
motion used `/move rel` board-axis deltas (chain FK + board axes, no
`camera_in_tool`).

## Setup / safety

- Bridge reachable, HOLD→REMOTE, armed, no fault, roll 68.04 throughout.
- Every leg dry-run first: joint delta 13.3°, roll 68.04→67.95 (same branch),
  tip inside keep-in. Leg printed before execution.
- Stop on 409 / branch flip / fault: enforced. Gripper untouched (0.76).
- Bridge restored to HOLD at the end.

## Trace (from reports/irl_servo_e5.json and console log)

| step | markers | gaze error mm | action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 2 | 90.7 | rel (+20, +20) |
| 1 | 0 (stale frame) | — | added stream-frame retry |
| resuming 0 | 2 | 67.3 | rel (+19.3, +20) |
| 1 | 2 | 46.5 | rel (+9.2, +20) |
| 2 | 3 | 26.6 | **409** "no reachable pose: off by 9.9 mm / 0.99 deg" → stopped |

Two clean legs closed the gaze error from 90 → 27 mm with the roll branch held.
The 409 is a workspace/approach limit: the camera had descended to tip z ≈ 33 mm
and the arm could not move further laterally at that height. Not a fault.

## Final observation

The last wrist frame shows the e5 piece visually centred. A numeric read of the
*square centre* pixel was ~110 px from image centre — expected, because a
standing piece's centroid projects outward from its base. Centring the piece on
screen is not the same as putting the jaw tip over its base.

## What this proves / does not prove

Proves: mount-free PnP perception drives a real closed loop on the live rig;
two `rel` legs executed with the branch held; the 409 guard stopped cleanly.
Does not prove: a grasp. IRL perception is weak (2–5 markers, 1–3 px RMS), and
a grasp needs the jaw-tip-over-piece-base geometry plus explicit gripper
authorisation — neither was in scope here.

## Next if continued

- Recover reach: back the camera up (dz>0) or change approach before the final
  lateral leg; or accept the residual since visual centring is achieved.
- For a real grasp: define the tip-to-piece-base target (not the standing
  centroid) and get explicit gripper-actuation approval.
