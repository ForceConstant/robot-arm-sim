## Goal and result

Ten known board placements were applied **physically** to the MuJoCo board
body (mocap `pos` x/y/z and `quat` yaw), then recovered with the production
marker plan and the printed ChArUco face. Truth is the applied transform.

**The multi-pose `/scan` fusion is inside 2 mm XY and 1° yaw at every
placement (worst 0.22 mm / 0.048°).** The board plane's z, fitted from the same
captures, is stable to **0.159 mm across the matrix** (shared fit) and
**0.191 mm** (per-view median), inside the 0.2 mm gate. Single-marker PnP is
not fit to be the bench procedure: its depth is biased **−3.1 mm** (median,
worst −9.4 mm) and it misses the 2 mm XY gate at one corner placement.

## Plane convention (settled)

Model datum (`chess_sim/config.py`, measured layout): mat 0.5 mm, slab
3.2 mm, so the **playing surface is 3.7 mm above the table (world 433.7 mm)**
and the arm base datum is 462.4 mm. A mat-mounted tag face is **0.5 mm above
the table (430.56 mm)**; the printed ChArUco face is **coplanar with the
playing surface (433.7 mm)**.

| estimator | returned plane | z semantics |
|---|---|---|
| multi-pose `/scan` fusion | board-frame origin on the **supplied marker plane**; x/y/yaw only | no z estimate. Registration's `board_z` is metadata. Pass `marker_z_m = 433.7` for the ChArUco face. |
| single-marker PnP (`/board/derive`) | **marker plane** used by the layout: ChArUco → playing surface; mat tags → tag face (0.5 mm above table) | returns the board-frame origin in the base frame; z is the tag plane. |
| offline median | same as single-marker PnP | same. |

**`live/board.json` z = 409.5** is the app's board-frame origin in the same
absolute frame as the model's **433.7**, i.e. it denotes the **playing
surface**, 24.2 mm below the twin's nominal surface and 21.0 mm below the
twin's ChArUco tag plane. A plane-convention error would be 3.2 mm, not 24 mm;
this is a bench table/mount datum or tape-measure difference
(`/board/assume` seeds z from camera height minus a measured standoff). Block 5
must compare relative offsets, not absolute z against the twin.

Note for comparisons: `board.json` x/y are **absolute** in the arm frame
(base at y = −217), while `/scan`'s `BoardOffset` is **base-relative**.

## Board-plane stability

Two independent views of the same quantity (robust `soft_l1`, 0.5 px scale):

- **Shared four-parameter plane fit per placement** (all marker-bearing views,
  one plane): z bias median **+0.042 mm**, spread across the ten placements
  **0.159 mm** → **inside the 0.2 mm gate**.
- **Per-view plane fit** (repeat the fit on each pose): z bias median
  −0.190 mm, spread across the matrix **0.191 mm** → inside.
  Within a placement the per-view spread is 0.102 mm median; worst 0.416 mm
  (`yaw−2.5`, whose only other view sees two tags — a weak depth lever arm).
- A z-only refit with x/y/yaw held fixed was **not** used: it couples in-plane
  error into depth (0.83 mm median spread) and is the wrong stability metric.

## Estimator comparison and named bench procedure

**Named procedure: multi-pose ChArUco fusion for x/y/yaw (`/scan`), plus a
multi-marker planar z fit over the same captures.**

- It is the only estimator inside 2 mm / 1° on every placement
  (worst 0.22 mm / 0.048°).
- Its plane z is stable to 0.16 mm across the matrix; single-tag z is biased
  by −3 mm and one sighting is 9.4 mm off.
- The offline median is a useful independent cross-check (x/y < 0.4 mm,
  yaw < 0.06°) but inherits the single-tag depth bias; it is not the z source.

The `/scan` endpoint must be paired with a z source: it does not estimate z.
The shared fit above is that source (same captures, no extra motion).

## Live config finding — the marker plane is stale for ChArUco

`live/live_config.json` has `layout_kind = "charuco"` but
`marker_z_m = 0.43056`, the **mat** plane. With that plane every one of the
ten placements fails the fusion's `MAX_RMS_PX = 1.2` gate:

| statistic with `marker_z = 0.43056` | value |
|---|---|
| reprojection RMS, all placements | 1.33–2.56 px (all rejected) |
| XY/yaw still recovered at a relaxed gate | 0.00–0.30 mm / ≤0.046° |

**Block 5 action:** set `marker_z_m` to `board_z_m` (0.4337) when
`layout_kind == "charuco"`. Otherwise `/scan` will report “board solve failed”
on a valid board.

## Defects found and fixed (all measured, not guessed)

1. **`chess_sim/fiducials.py` — `object_points()` crashed on every layout.**
   `MARKER_IDS` spans mat tags 0–4 and ChArUco 5–36, but a layout defines one
   face. Asking a mat layout for id 5 raised `unknown marker id 5` and took
   down `/scan`, `/board/derive` and the offline tool. It now skips ids the
   layout cannot describe, and `estimate_board_pose` builds its point-index
   map from the ids actually emitted.
2. **`fiducials.estimate_board_pose()` seed — wrong camera convention.**
   The per-view PnP seed ignored the OpenCV(+z)/MuJoCo(−z) flip and composed
   the transforms inverted, so the bounded refinement started ~180° away and
   half the placements failed with “board solve failed”. The seed now uses
   `base←camera @ [flip·R | flip·t]`; per-view median+3 MAD pruning was added
   (the docs already call robust pruning mandatory).
3. **`live/app.py` and `tools/calibrate_board_charuco.py` — same bug in the
   single-marker path.** Both inverted the PnP transform and skipped the axis
   flip. Before the fix the sim reported z **+1074 mm** and yaw **+20°** at a
   nominal board; after it, z **−3.2 mm** and yaw 0.01° (the residual is the
   single-tag depth bias).
4. **`chess_sim/charuco_face.py` — rendered tag was 19.906 mm, not 20 mm.**
   At 128 px/square the 20/28 ratio rounds to 91 px. The 0.47 % size error was
   a ~0.7 px reprojection floor and 0.32 mm plane-z spread. Default is now
   224 px/square, where the 20 mm tag is exactly 160 px and every edge is on
   an integer pixel; residual fell to ~0.3 px and the plane spread to
   0.159 mm.
5. **Calibration file mismatch (not changed).** `.private/phase11d/
   calibration.json` is the 2026-11-17 real hand-eye mount
   (`handeye-2026-11-17T12:12`, fx 312.77, camera 64 mm off-axis), while the
   sim wrist camera is the 36° lens (fx 738.64, camera 80 mm forward). The sim
   proof uses `reports/block3_sim_calibration.json`, exported from the model
   (`tools/export_wrist_calibration.py`, chain max error 0.000 mm). The live
   intrinsics/mount consistency must be re-checked at the bench.
