## Traps found today (all cost real time)

- The detector only runs while a **stream client is attached**; `/state` reads
  `markers 0 / starting` with nobody watching. Hold a `/stream` reader open when
  sampling detections.
- `rel` moves are in the **tool frame**, not board axes: `rel dz` walked the tip
  ~50 mm sideways at this wrist pitch. Use absolute `xyz` legs.
- The tip hangs ~8 mm below the commanded z; step with **absolute levels**, not
  `readback + n`.
- A printed-face residual is worthless for the sim (its board texture and the
  reference face are different images). Subtract an **empty render** of the same
  scene; residual centroids then track offset at 0.999 correlation.
- The sim board-body frame is **180° + ~5 mm** from the marker-layout frame.
- GPU: sustained compute with **KDE Wayland running** hard-locks the board
  (three times today). From `multi-user.target` it ran three for three. Filed as
  MastaG/linux-cachyos-bc250#15.
